Who We Are

Kannan Musical Mart

Kannan Musical Mart is one of Chennai's trusted musical instrument stores, serving musicians, students, schools, and professionals across the city. From a curious beginner's first instrument to a touring artist's stage-ready gear, we've built our name on one simple idea: sell what you'd want your own family to play.

Our Story

From a Small Music Shop to a Trusted Destination

Kannan Musical Mart began as a modest shop with a simple ambition — to put good instruments into the hands of people who genuinely loved music. Word travelled the way it always does in this city: a student told a friend, a friend told their teacher, and slowly, a small counter of instruments became a destination musicians planned their visits around.

Growth never meant losing the personal touch. Every guitar is still checked by hand. Every harmonium is still tested before it leaves the store. That habit, more than anything else, is what turned first-time customers into regulars, and regulars into a community.

Today, the same values that opened our doors continue to guide us — honest advice, fair pricing, and instruments worth trusting on stage or in the practice room.
